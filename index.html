Cube Dash — How to Play

Survive as long as you can. Fly through portals to change game mode. Your best score is saved automatically.

Controls

  • Tap (Space / W / ↑ / Click / Tap): jump, flip, flap, or use an orb you're touching.
  • Hold: auto-jump in Cube/Robot, fly & steer in Ship/Wave/Swing, or ride a dash orb.
  • E: toggle the bot — let the robot play, or take over yourself (any difficulty).

Game Modes

  • Cube — jump over spikes.
  • Ship — hold to rise, release to fall.
  • Wave — hold = up 45°, release = down 45°.
  • Robot — hold for a higher jump.
  • Ball — tap to flip gravity & roll the other surface.
  • UFO — each tap is a little flap upward.
  • Spider — tap to teleport to the other surface.
  • Swing — hold flips gravity up, release flips it down.

Dual

A dual portal splits you into two mirrored players — one on the floor, one on the ceiling. They share your input (mirrored), and both must survive. Hit another dual portal to merge back.

Orbs (tap while touching)

  • Yellow — normal jump
  • Pink — small jump
  • Red — big jump
  • Black — slam toward gravity
  • Green — flip gravity + jump
  • Blue (gravity) — reverse gravity
  • Green dash — hold to dash straight ahead
  • Spider — flip gravity + teleport

Portals

  • Mode — switch game mode · Dual — split / merge two players.
  • Gravity — flips gravity up/down.
  • Mini / Big — shrink or restore your size.
  • Speed — dashed neon gates (»») change scroll speed x0.8–x2.0.

Pads (auto-activate on contact)

  • Yellow — bounce up · Pink — small · Red — big launch
  • Blue — reverse gravity · Spider — flip + teleport

Debug

Press F to toggle hitboxes. Press E to switch between playing yourself and watching the bot.
